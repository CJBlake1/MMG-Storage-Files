You will need to edit the values of the usage, category, value, nominals and mins
of all the MMG types to your liking and validate your edited files. 

Then

Add the following below to your cfgeconomycore.xml under </defaults>:

    <ce folder="MMG_Storage">		
		<file name="Craftable.xml" type="types" />
		<file name="Crates_Cases_Cabinets_Boxes.xml" type="types" />
		<file name="GunRacks_GunWall.xml" type="types" />
                <file name="Lockers.xml" type="types" />
		<file name="Misc.xml" type="types" />
		<file name="Statics.xml" type="types" />
	</ce>

Finally, add the MMG_Storage folder to your map MP Mission folder.
